Meet Your Therapist

Hi, I'm Mesha.

In today's society we've grown accustomed to taking something for pain — a prescription, an injection, sometimes surgery. But the root of the issue isn't always understood. Instead, a bandage gets placed over it and we expect it to go away. It's time to take control of your health.

Taking the holistic approach and helping your body heal naturally is what we're all about. Massage isn't only for relaxation — it reduces stress and pain, eliminates toxins, prevents injury, improves circulation and flexibility, and helps alleviate depression and anxiety.

At Essential Massage by Mesha, I believe massage is essential to living a healthy lifestyle. You are never just another appointment — every session is shaped around what you need that day.
