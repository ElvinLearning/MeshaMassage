I’m new to massage. Which service should I book?

A 60-minute Swedish Massage is a lovely place to start: smooth, gliding strokes with firm but gentle pressure. If you have a specific area of pain, the Rehab Therapy Massage is built for that. Still unsure? Text Mesha and she’ll help you choose.

Is Essential Massage welcoming to everyone?

Yes. Every body, every age, every gender. Every session is customized to your goals, comfort and pressure preferences, and you are never just another appointment.

Do you offer prenatal massage?

Yes, in 60-minute ($130) and 90-minute ($170) sessions. It’s performed in a supported side-lying position with extra pillows for comfort, using smooth Swedish strokes and gentle pressure.

How do packages and series work?

You prepay for a set number of sessions and the price per session drops, up to 33% off the single-session rate. Series are redeemable against the matching service. See every series.

Can I buy a gift certificate?

Absolutely. Any amount from $10 to $2,000, redeemable toward any service. Request one here.
